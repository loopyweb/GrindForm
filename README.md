# AI Pushup Form Checker & Tracker

This tool uses **Computer Vision** to analyze your pushup form in real-time. It tracks your joints, counts your reps, and provides instant feedback on your posture using Google's MediaPipe and OpenCV.

 Features
- Rep Counter: Automatically counts completed reps using a state-machine logic.
- Form Analysis: Checks for elbow depth (90-degree rule) and hip alignment.
- Real-time Feedback:Displays on-screen prompts like "Go Deeper" or "Fix Your Hips."
